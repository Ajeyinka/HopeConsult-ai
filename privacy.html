1. Who We Are

Hope Impact Consulting Limited ("Hope Impact", "we", "us") provides grant writing, funding strategy, proposal development, government contracting support, capture development, and nonprofit consulting. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected and used through hopeimpactconsulting.com and related enquiries. Contact us at info@hopeimpactconsulting.online with privacy questions.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or request a consultation, we may collect your name, organization, email address, optional phone number, organization type, service interests, opportunity details, funding or contract value, deadlines, and messages. If you choose to upload a solicitation, grant guideline, or supporting document, it may contain additional personal or confidential information. Please share only what is reasonably necessary for your enquiry.

3. How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, assess potential engagements, arrange consultations, prepare proposals or scopes of work, deliver agreed services, maintain client relationships, and meet applicable legal obligations. We may use limited technical information to maintain website security and troubleshoot performance. We do not promise grant or contract awards.

4. Legal Grounds and Your Choices

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on your request to take steps toward a contract, performance of an agreement, legitimate business interests, compliance with legal obligations, or consent when required. You may choose not to provide optional information, but that may limit our ability to assess your enquiry.

5. Documents and Confidential Information

Proposal documents, financial materials, program descriptions, and other files you submit may be sensitive. We use them to evaluate or carry out the requested services and restrict access to people who reasonably need them for that purpose. Please do not submit Social Security numbers, payment-card details, medical records, or other highly sensitive data through the website contact form unless we have agreed on an appropriate secure transfer method.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers that support website hosting, email, scheduling, document storage, security, and client communications, subject to their relevant terms and safeguards.
